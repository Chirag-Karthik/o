2. What challenges did you face and how did you develop?
 * Slow Queries and Heavy Reports: Working with large datasets initially slowed down SQL queries and Power BI refresh times. I developed my skills by simplifying my query logic, cleaning up data models, and writing lighter DAX measures to keep reports fast.
 * Managing Tight Deadlines with Urgent Requests: Balancing urgent, deep-dive root cause investigations alongside routine daily reports was difficult. I improved by setting clearer priorities, managing my time better, and automating basic checks so reports were never late.
 * Connecting Data to Financial Impact: Estimating the exact dollar ($) impact of metric changes took time to master. I worked directly with business partners to better understand their daily operations, which helped me translate data findings into real business impact.
